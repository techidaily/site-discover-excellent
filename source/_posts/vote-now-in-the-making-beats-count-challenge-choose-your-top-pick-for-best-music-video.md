## Vote Now in the Making Beats Count Challenge! Choose Your Top Pick for Best Music Video

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/makingbeatscount-contest.jpg?resize=530%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/makingbeatscount-contest.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "makingbeatscount-contest")

##### Voting has begun, it’s time to pick your favorite Making Beats Count music video!

![brrpcdj](https://i1.wp.com/www.pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/brrpcdj-300x300.jpg?resize=300%2C300&ssl=1)PCDJ teamed up with [Beats Rhymes & Relief](http://beatsrhymesandrelief.org/) (_a non-profit organization utilizing the arts to raise awareness and support for worldwide humanitarian relief efforts_) back in September 2013 to kick off the [Making Beats Count Contest](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).

Over the past year Beats Rhymes & Relief traveled around the US hosting free block party style events designed to bring artists and industry to communities in need to inspire positive actions for social good.

In an effort to raise awareness about the importance of positive messaging, community service and mentor-ship they brought together talented producers, emcee’s, graffiti artists, break dancers and DJs to participate in this social good campaign that has influenced thousands of youth across the nation.

5 major cities came together to produce 5 new tracks and videos: _Dallas, Chicago, D.C., N.Y.C. and L.A._ We we truly amazed with the submissions, the tracks are fantastic and the videos top notch. Each participating city did a bang-up job, casting a vote for the best is a difficult choice.

#### LET THE VOTING BEGIN (Voting Ends NOV 30th 2014 | Winner Will Be Announced DEC 1ST)

Take a few moments to [watch each entry here and vote for your favorite](http://beatsrhymesandrelief.org/contest/). The video with the most votes will receive the Making Beats Count “Peoples Choice” Award and each contributing member will be invited to perform the song at the One World Celebrity Benefit Concert in Washington DC this winter.

The winning video producer will receive a copy of [PCDJ DEX 3 DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and an American Audio VMS2 DJ controller as pictured below, .
