## Discover the Latest Karaoke Enhancements in PCDJ's DEX Version 3.10 and Beyond - A Comprehensive Video Guide

DEX 3.10 ([currently in public beta as a release candidate](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)) was launched yesterday and comes with a series of new karaoke features designed to make it easier than ever to host a professional karaoke show.

In the video above we demonstrate the new karaoke singer rotation list, singer and song history, and more. As we noted in yesterdays release notes, this is only the first in a series of scheduled updates for DEX 3 that will beef up the karaoke feature set.
