Best of all — _the subscription is legal for live performance in commercial venues._

PCDJ Karaoki is our best karaoke software for hosting, and designed for karaoke hosts that demand the best singer and song rotation management (now with [SongbookDB](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) support). When using Karaoke Cloud Pro, KJs sign into the subscription through Karaoki and receive immediate access to over 8,000 top-quality karaoke tracks right in the library from the best manufacturers in the business. The included karaoke manufacturer’s are: **Digitrax**, **DK**, **Sunfly**, **SBI** and **Stage Stars**.

New karaoke tracks are added all the time, and since the files are downloaded to your local hard drive you don’t require internet access when using the subscription — you only have to sync with the online server once a month to verify successful payment. This means you can use [PCDJ Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) with the [Karaoke Cloud Pro](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) service for 30 days offline at a time.
