New karaoke tracks are added all the time, and since the files are downloaded to your local hard drive you don’t require internet access when using the subscription — you only have to sync with the online server once a month to verify successful payment. This means you can use [PCDJ Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) with the [Karaoke Cloud Pro](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) service for 30 days offline at a time.
