When using Karaoke Cloud Pro, KJs sign into the subscription through Karaoki and receive immediate access to over 8,000 top-quality karaoke tracks right in the library from the best manufacturers in the business. New karaoke tracks are added all the time, and since the files are downloaded to your local hard drive you don’t require internet access when using the subscription — you only have to sync with the online server once a month to verify successful payment. This means you can use [PCDJ Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) with the [Karaoke Cloud Pro](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) service for 30 days offline at a time.
